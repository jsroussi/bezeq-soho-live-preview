# BEZEQ AIRLINES mobile travel adaptation

The owner's reference calls for the visual confidence and convenient navigation of a travel app, applied to the existing competition. Prior authorization delegates routine design choices and asks to continue through implementation; no new business-rule or production decision is needed.

Use the existing approved Paris image as a substantial mobile masthead with readable Hebrew title and visible telecommunications branding. A white journey panel overlaps its lower edge, showing the current destination, approved dates, nine-stop progress and a clear personal-entry action. Follow it with direct links and a horizontally scrollable itinerary. Conceal all future destination identities. Desktop retains its existing composition.

Provide a five-item mobile bottom navigation with safe-area padding, route state and accessible names. Signed-in users reach their dashboard, ranking, journey and announcements directly; an accessible sheet contains rules, home, role switching and authorized management routes. Public users get home, journey, updates, rules and login. Preserve every existing route. Keep management screens focused on work; give representative and manager overviews a compact destination heading and reduce excess boarding-pass space without obscuring sales, stamps or eligibility.

Retain native DOM/CSS/WAAPI, Simpler fonts, telecom navy #20294D, blue #005EB8, cyan #00AEEF and white. No new libraries, imagery, booking controls, invented destinations, rules or prizes. Maintain visible synthetic labeling. No changes to Claude's preview, assets, branches/worktrees, production or canonical business/auth code.

Alternative considered: a landing-only reskin would leave routine navigation difficult; a full app replacement would exceed the request. Adapt the existing shared mobile shell and landing while keeping working desktop and operational screens.

Verify Chromium at 320/360/390/430/600/768/1440, short landscape and enlarged text; test bottom navigation, keyboard sheet behavior, role scopes, actual scrolling, dialogs, no fixed-bar occlusion, future secrecy, reduced motion and all existing suites. Screenshots must be reviewed. Real iOS/Android hardware is unavailable and is a documented limit, not a claim of hardware verification.

Publish only owned preview files and a new airlines-review-mobile namespace, retaining every existing unrelated and legacy Git blob. Recheck the actual gh-pages head and Pages source before publication. Record source/deployment commits and the outcome of a fresh public browser attempt.
